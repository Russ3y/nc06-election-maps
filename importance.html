How importance is calculated

Each precinct's total 2026 votes ÷ 273,738 districtwide votes. Importance rank compares 195 geographically matched precinct reporting rows.

Reporting limitation

Early, absentee and other separately reported votes are included in the district total, but cannot be geographically assigned to individual precincts from this source. Uncolored areas are not zero-vote precincts.
