01 / Overview

Registration and election results tell different stories.

McDowell received 133,737 votes (48.86%), while Jefferson received 140,001 (51.14%). Republican registration is the largest single affiliation in the district, but unaffiliated registration is nearly as large. Registration figures cannot establish individual voting behavior.
